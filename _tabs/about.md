## Who am I?
Hi! I'm Andrea, a student with a growing interest in dentistry and the intersection of science, precision and creativity.

I've always enjoyed working with my hands, whether through drawing, experimenting with different art mediums or taking on creative projects. I find satisfaction in paying attention to the little details and turning ideas into something tangible. This appreciation for hands-on work is one of the things that drew me towards dentistry.

## Why Dentistry?
I'm fascinated by how dentistry combines scientific knowledge with practical skill, from understanding the intricacies of oral anatomy to using precision and dexterity to restore function and improve someone's confidence in their smile. Through my experiences at Tooth Matters, I've also had the opportunity to observe clinical procedures and gain a better appreciation for the work that goes on behind the scenes of a dental practice.

## Other interests!
Outside of dentistry and science, I enjoy art, exploring creative ideas and learning about things that pique my curiosity. This website is a space for me to document my experiences, explore developments in dentistry and share what I learn along the way. I'm excited to see where this journey takes me!
